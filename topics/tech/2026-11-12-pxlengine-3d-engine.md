## Summary
pxlengine (`pxl`): a headless 3D and VFX engine built for AI agents. An agent writes a shot as JSON, runs `pxl render`, looks at the PNG and revises; no editor. GPU path tracing, glTF models and HDRI skies, custom Slang shaders, millions of GPU agents and FLIP fluid, and CG-into-footage compositing with tracked cameras. Outputs PNG/EXR, MP4 or ProRes.
