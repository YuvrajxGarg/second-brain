## Summary
Vinyl for Windows: a live desktop wallpaper that turns whatever is playing (Spotify, YouTube, any app Windows reports) into a spinning three.js turntable with the album art on the label and a cover card beside it. Licensed with signed keys, auto-updates from a public releases repo. Inspired by Vinyl for Mac.
