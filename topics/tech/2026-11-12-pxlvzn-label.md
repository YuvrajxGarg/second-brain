## Summary
PXLVZN (pxlvzn.in) is Yuvraj's own label for free, open-source desktop apps and side projects. The PXL app installs and updates every PXLVZN app; each app gets its own subdomain site (dwn, stm, lut, hop, canvas) in one shared design: near-black surfaces, hairline strokes, white primary buttons and one lime accent. games.pxlvzn.in lists his party games (Punchline so far), and this second brain lives at brainy.pxlvzn.in.
