## Summary
A tip from Jake Silverstein (@lazyjakee) for solo founders building mobile apps with Claude: have it build in native Swift, with no user accounts and no cloud storage. Keeping all data on the device is the safest setup when you're one person, since there's no auth or backend to secure or maintain.
