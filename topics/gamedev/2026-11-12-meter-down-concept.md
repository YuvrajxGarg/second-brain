## Summary
Meter Down: a daily Mumbai taxi game concept. You drive a kaali-peeli for six minutes with one AI passenger who has a hot take; read the person, speak in your own words, and at drop-off see how others handled them, the feed that shaped them and the real reporting behind the take. Weekly Driver Card shows your own patterns. The site has a scripted walkthrough, a grant pitch and a 12-month roadmap.
