## Summary
Runaway Light Rig: a rhythm game where every note you hit fires a light rig in a dystopian concert set (kick wash, strobes, piano spot, halo, moving heads, lasers). Load your own track; notes come from its frequency bands. Easy to hard, play or watch mode, audio offset.
