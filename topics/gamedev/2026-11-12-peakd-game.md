## Summary
PEAKD is a daily "when did it peak?" browser game: five things a day (slang, series, films, games, viral moments), 30 seconds each to pick the month between 2015 and 2025 when it peaked; closer months score more, up to 5,000. Every entry uses a real frozen monthly interest series, never invented curves. Editorial redesign: cream paper, ink condensed serif, a tick ruler and rolling year numerals.
