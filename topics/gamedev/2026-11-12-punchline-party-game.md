## Summary
Punchline: an online comedy party game for 2 to 8 friends on their own phones. The game sets up a joke, everyone writes the punchline, and an AI judge decides who killed and who bombed. Room codes, no sign-up, flavours from clean to after dark, a pass-and-play mode, a global Daily Drop prompt and leaderboards.
