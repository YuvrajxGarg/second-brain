## Summary
games.revolio.media: the Revolio Labs home as a scroll-driven three.js side-scroller along a window ledge. A TV and radio, a game shelf where cartridges slot into a console, a cutting mat of experiments (a dev board, a desk robot arm, the studio), an instant-photo booth, sticky notes and switchable lighting (golden hour, daylight, blue hour, night). Plain ES modules, no build step. An earlier version was a 3D room.
