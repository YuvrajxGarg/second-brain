## Summary
A free browser cricket game: spin a wheel to land on a real World Cup squad (e.g. West Indies 1979, India 2011), draft players into the batting order where they actually batted, then watch a ball-by-ball simulated chase of 500 in 50 overs with live commentary and a final scorecard. Odds depend on your top-7 batting, your 7 biggest hitters and your 4 best bowlers; no keeper or captain costs a small penalty. Unofficial fan project.
