## Summary
A free daily challenge game in the style of Wordle: guess the day's international cricketer in 10 tries. Each guess reveals country, role, batting and bowling style, debut year and matches played, with green for a match, orange for close, and arrows pointing toward the answer. Also has practice rounds and leaderboards.
