## Summary
SLANGDAQ, "the culture exchange": a stock market for internet slang. Players get 1,000 play tokens, go long on slang they think is rising ($RIZZ, $DELU, $SKBD...) or short what's "cooked", claim a daily token drop and climb a leaderboard. Play money only.
