## Summary
A concept bible for a contained detective game: you have one night to find Niko, a background NPC who witnessed a deliberate deletion, in one living city district. Every NPC remembers how you treated them and may withhold clues or block routes. Covers story, district, obstacles, systems, production plan and a writers' room.
