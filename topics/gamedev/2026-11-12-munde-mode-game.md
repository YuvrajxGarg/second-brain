## Summary
Munde Mode: a parody top-down Mumbai FDA raid simulator, the playable form of his Tukaram Munde idea. Each day you get an inspection brief, start at a restaurant door as Munde, check fridges, thermometers and burners, log 3 to 5 violations, pick an action and face the Bombay High Court. Four restaurant tiers (street stall, budget udupi or biryani place, Irani family cafe, fine dining). Characters were made from GPT Image references via Hyper3D and rigged on Mixamo; rooms and props are procedural three.js.
