## Summary
Freddy Fang's checklist, drawn from app experiments across 78k apps, of what to try before giving up on an app. On the product side: rewrite onboarding, test paywall prices, plans and trials, add a short calculation screen before the paywall, win back users who bounce, and focus on one funnel metric at a time. On distribution: post 100+ short-form videos, test hooks, run several creator accounts, use UGC creators and spark ads, run Apple Search Ads on competitor names, and build one feature people screenshot on their own.

## Notes
Full post (read via Social Reader, text only):

Product & Paywall:
- Rewritten your onboarding at least 5 times and checked where users drop off?
- Tested your paywall with different price points, weekly vs annual, and trial vs no-trial?
- Added a 3-second calculation/loading screen before showing the paywall?
- Shown a different offer to people who bounced the next time they open the app?
- Watched a stranger use your app in person?
- Added a viral loop in your product?
- Focused on the improvement of ONE metric at a time (install -> trial, trial -> paid)
- Had Claude dig through your competitor's one star reviews and improved them

Distribution:
- Posted 100+ short-form videos across TikTok, Reels, FB, & YT Shorts?
- Tested 20+ different hooks and cut everything that did not hold past 3 seconds?
- Ran multiple creator accounts instead of relying on one page?
- Reposted your top 5% winning videos with new hooks?
- Paid different UGC creators and turned the winning clips into paid ads?
- Ran paid spark ads on ALL organic videos
- Ran Apple Search Ads on competitor names and intent keywords?
- Built ONE feature so good that people screenshot it without you asking?
