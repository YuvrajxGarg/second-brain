## Summary
A thread by greg_buildz on growing a new app from zero downloads with no ad spend, using organic TikTok slideshows. The steps: find your audience by saving viral slideshows in your niche, warm up the account for a day or two, post consistently even when the first posts flop, put real thought into the first-slide hook, and end with a soft CTA (the app logo, never "download" or the app name). When a post wins, study it and make five new variations. He says this got his own app to 14,000+ downloads.

## Notes
Read via Social Reader (8-post thread, text only). Steps in his words:
1. Figuring out your audience: search niche keywords on TikTok, save every viral slideshow, interact with them.
2. Warming up the account: 1-2 days max, save and interact a bit, don't bot-like or TikTok restricts you.
3. First slideshow post: may take a week or two to get views; stay consistent, add slideshows to your story.
4. Hook is everything: first slide decides virality; emotional and relatable, with a matching sound.
5. Post consistently + good CTA: app logo at the end, never say "download" or the app name.
6. Double down on winners: study the hook, topic and engagement of the winner, make 5 variations.
7. Takeaways: grew his own app to 14,000+ downloads this way; offers it as a service.
