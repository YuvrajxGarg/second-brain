## Summary
Ledger: Revolio's post-shoot expense app for a production house. Invoices are collected (including a Gmail scan), checked against the house billing format with LLM extraction, sent through multi-tier approval, dispatched to accounts and confirmed as paid, all built around the closing sheet. Vendors, reimbursements and email reminders too.
