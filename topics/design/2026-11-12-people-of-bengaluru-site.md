## Summary
People of Bengaluru ("Walk the city. Meet the people who design it.") is a website that's a walkable, stylised 3D Bengaluru where every character is a real designer or builder. You wander areas like Koramangala and HSR Layout, find people, see what each one is building and say hi. It works as a mobile web app. Saved for the idea (a city-as-directory of real people) and the website itself.
