## Summary
TapMac ("tap your MacBook, start talking") is a macOS app that turns taps on the MacBook into actions: one tap for on-device voice dictation, two taps for AI text actions on selected text (explain, fix grammar, rephrase, summarise), three taps for clipboard history, plus smart modes like mute on calls and play/pause video. A menu-bar mascot called Tappy shows the app's state. It's private (on-device, no account), Apple silicon only, and sells for a one-time $7.99 launch price (later $20) with no subscription. Saved both as a cool app and as a landing page to copy when building something similar.

Website: black, minimal layout; bold headlines with italic emphasis; Tappy appears in many expressions; a tap-count diagram explains the one, two and three tap actions; a looping demo video, a wall of social-media testimonials, directory badges and a single-price section ("Get 100% back" for posting about it, 50% off code for India).
