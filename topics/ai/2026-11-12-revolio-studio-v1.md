## Summary
The first Revolio Studio: an AI creative studio for image, video and 3D generation in one app, with Google sign-in, prompts with reference images, start/end frame locking, @-mentions of gallery images and a live-polling gallery. It later got a Magnific-style redesign (projects, templates, a resources bank, night and bright modes) before the rebuild as the Revolio Labs app.
