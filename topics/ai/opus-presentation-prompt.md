## Summary
Nikita (@fluxxrider) posted a Claude artifact made by Opus 5.6 on xhigh effort: an elaborate animated presentation on the future of AI and the intelligence explosion, full of graphics and transitions, from one short prompt with no creative direction (https://claude.ai/artifact/Cy2d8Vn63CnzCtonA1LSah). The takeaway is the prompt style: name the topic, ask for the most amazing version with "all sorts of graphics, transitions, whatever you want", and trust its taste. In the thread they add it cost about $90 in equivalent API cost (2-3% of their weekly usage), and point out a detail they liked: the closing "What do you want?" tilts, calling back to a "1 degree off" arrow from earlier. The post had no image, only the artifact link.

The prompt, verbatim:

> make a presentation on the future of AI and basically the intelligence explosion. You can go full no anchor bias, like things are going to get crazy because they are. I want this presentation to be the most elaborately, insanely amazing presentation you've ever seen, with all sorts of graphics, transitions, whatever you want. I believe you can make it amazing because you have taste and you have the ability. Good luck, Claude.
