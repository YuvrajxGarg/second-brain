### [Sleep alarm UI](../topics/design/2026-11-13-sleep-alarm-ui.md)

`style-ref` · design · <https://x.com/PostiauxCharles/status/2121248954260267411>

- **Why saved:** cool design
- **Style:** dark minimal ui, dot-matrix data viz, monospace type, single blue accent, scrubbable timeline
- **Tags:** #visual-design #ui #data-viz #inspiration

A 20-second UI concept by Charles Postiaux (@PostiauxCharles) for a sleep-cycle-based alarm. A dark screen plots the last 30 nights as dot-matrix bands for awake, REM, light and deep sleep, and you drag a vertical "up by" line along the timeline; a blue line shows when it actually rings (a few minutes early, timed to light sleep). Below it, presets for weekdays, gym days and weekend each show their up-by time, ring time and why. Saved as a dark data-viz UI reference.

`style-ref` · design · <https://x.com/kshvbgde/status/2120782192385355974>

- **Style:** dark compact utility card, glowing dot-matrix storage bar, colour-coded categories, plain-language helper copy, particle dissolve on delete
- **Tags:** #ui #desktop-app #visual-design #inspiration

A short demo from keshav (@kshvbgde) of a Mac storage scanner: "storage scanner so good you'll want to fill your disk." One dark card shows used space (e.g. 357.8 GB of 512 GB) as a glowing dot-matrix bar split into colour-coded categories (Apps, Developer, Photos, Movies, Documents, Music, System data, Caches, Trash). - **Why saved:** This is a cool app, and the website is also cool for building something similar
- **Style:** black minimal landing page, bold headlines with italic emphasis, expressive mascot, tap-count diagram, testimonial wall
- **Tags:** #desktop-app #website #inspiration #reference

TapMac ("tap your MacBook, start talking") is a macOS app that turns taps on the MacBook into actions: one tap for on-device voice dictation, two taps for AI text actions on selected text (explain, fix grammar, rephrase, summarise), three taps for clipboard history, plus smart modes like mute on calls and play/pause video. A menu-bar mascot called Tappy shows the app's state. It's private (on-device, no account), Apple silicon only, and sells for a one-time $7.99 launch price (later $20) with no subscription. Saved both as a cool app and as a landing page to copy when building something similar.

A free browser cricket game: spin a wheel to land on a real World Cup squad (e.g. West Indies 1979, India 2011), draft players into the batting order where they actually batted, then watch a ball-by-ball simulated chase of 500 in 50 overs with live commentary and a final scorecard.
