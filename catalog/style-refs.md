### [Captcha launch film](../topics/design/2026-11-11-captcha-launch-film.md)

`style-ref` · design · <https://x.com/AustinBauwens/status/2119800432797118696>

- **Why saved:** cool visuals, launch film
- **Style:** chunky flat pixel art, orange and blue palette, pixel cursors and ui, launch film motion
- **Tags:** #visual-design #pixel-art #inspiration

A 17-second animated launch film shared by Austin Bauwens, titled "Captchas aren't what you think they are". It argues that solving captchas has been unpaid AI training, from labelling people to steering self-driving cars. The look is flat chunky pixel-art in orange, blue and periwinkle, with captcha grids, bounding boxes, pixel cursors and a car driving a top-down map. Directed by Sam Essanoussi, with design and animation by Justin De Leon and Connor Roy-Shaw. Saved as motion and visual design inspiration.

### [500-0 game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-09-thechase500-cricket-chase-game.md)

`game-ref` · gamedev · <https://500-0.com>

- **Why saved:** fun cricket browser games
- **Tags:** #browser-game #cricket #game-inspiration #to-try

A free browser cricket game: spin a wheel to land on a real World Cup squad (e.g. West Indies 1979, India 2011), draft players into the batting order where they actually batted, then watch a ball-by-ball simulated chase of 500 in 50 overs with live commentary and a final scorecard. Odds depend on your top-7 batting, your 7 biggest hitters and your 4 best bowlers; no keeper or captain costs a small penalty. Unofficial fan project.
