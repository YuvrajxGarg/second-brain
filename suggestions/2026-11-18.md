## 1. The Outsider on Punchline rails

**Build:** Turn The Outsider concept into a playable game by reusing Punchline's phone rooms (room codes, no sign-up, 2 to 8 players). Everyone gets the same secret slang word except one Outsider, each player types one clue on their phone, then the room votes. Pull the secret words from SLANGDAQ's ticker list ($RIZZ, $DELU, $SKBD...) so it's instantly funny and desi-online.
**From:** [The Outsider concept](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-the-outsider-concept.md) + [Punchline party game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-punchline-party-game.md) + [SLANGDAQ game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-slangdaq-game.md)
**Why it fits:** a party game with one clear hook, built on two things he already shipped, so it's a weekend, not a month.
**First step today:** fork Punchline's room/lobby code into a new Next.js route, swap the "write a punchline" round for "one-word clue", and hardcode 20 SLANGDAQ tickers as the word list.
**Status:** suggested

## 2. AI shot finisher

**Build:** A one-drop finishing pass for AI clips in client edits: upscale the clip with RelayVSR, then run Lut against the real A-cam reference so it matches resolution and colour in one go. Ship it as a new mode in Lut (or a PXL hub app) with a before/after split view.
**From:** [Lut colour matcher](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-lut-colour-matcher.md) + [RelayVSR video upscaler](../topics/ai/2026-11-13-relayvsr-video-upscaler.md)
**Why it fits:** Lut was made for matching AI shots to real footage; upscaling is the other half of that same job at Revolio.
**First step today:** take one AI shot from a recent Revolio edit, upscale it in ComfyUI with RelayVSR X2, run it through Lut against the A-cam frame, and drop both versions on the timeline to judge if it holds up.
**Status:** suggested
