**From:** [Wedding co-op game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-wedding-co-op-game.md) + [Stick It Up stickers](../topics/design/2026-11-13-stick-it-up-stickers.md) + [Revolio Labs](../topics/business/2026-11-12-revolio-labs.md)

## 2. ARC remix reel in pxlengine

**Build:** Have an agent rebuild 4 or 5 of ARC's free Creative Commons 3D scenes as pxlengine shot JSON (glTF in, HDRI sky, path-traced), give each one a short camera move, and cut them into a 20-second reel: "my engine, an agent, and ARC's open art", with ARC credited. It is a real stress test of pxlengine on glossy gold and pastel materials, and a showcase post for the engine.
**From:** [pxlengine 3D engine](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-pxlengine-3d-engine.md) + [ARC 3D art dataset](../topics/design/2026-11-10-arc-3d-art-dataset.md)
**Why it fits:** pxlengine exists so agents can make shots by writing JSON; ARC literally invited people to remix with agents, and the edit is his day job.
**First step today:** download one ARC project, export it to glTF, and get a single `pxl render` PNG of it under an HDRI.
**Status:** suggested
