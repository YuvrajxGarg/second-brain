## 1. Dot-matrix disk sweeper

**Build:** A first version of his disk cleanup app for Mac: one dark card with a glowing dot-matrix storage bar split by category (caches, Xcode/node junk, Trash, downloads), plain-language hover text and one button per category. Clearing a category makes its dots scatter as particles. Ship it with a one-page black landing site in the TapMac style (bold headline with italic emphasis, a tiny mascot, one price).
**From:** [Disk cleanup app](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-disk-cleanup-app.md) + [Storage scanner UI](../topics/design/2026-11-12-storage-scanner-ui.md) + [TapMac trackpad app](../topics/design/2026-11-12-tapmac-trackpad-app.md)
**Why it fits:** Turns one of his own open ideas into a PXLVZN utility, in the polished dark utility look he saved, and fits next to DWN, STM and Vinyl as a paid or free desktop tool.
**First step today:** Write a scanner (Swift or Python) that sizes `~/Library/Caches`, `~/.Trash`, `~/Library/Developer` and `node_modules` folders, then draw the result as a canvas dot-matrix bar with one colour per category.
**Status:** suggested

## 2. Munde Mode transfer map

**Build:** A walkable street hub for Munde Mode, in the People of Bengaluru spirit: between raid days, Munde walks down a stylised Indian street of Sharma Sweets-style shopfronts (bottle green, marigold, warm light), and the shop he walks into becomes today's inspection. Each "transfer" order moves him to a new city street (Mumbai, Pune, Nagpur), which doubles as the level select and a running joke about his real-life transfers.
**From:** [Munde Mode game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-munde-mode-game.md) + [People of Bengaluru](../topics/design/2026-11-12-people-of-bengaluru-site.md) + [Sharma Sweets 3D style](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-sharma-sweets-3d-shopfront.md)
**Why it fits:** Moves his in-progress Tukaram Munde game forward instead of starting fresh, with the desi cartoon 3D look and the walkable-city idea he called "insane".
**First step today:** Block out one 6-shop street in three.js with a follow camera, make each shop door a trigger that loads the existing restaurant raid, and give one shop a proper Sharma Sweets-style facade.
**Status:** suggested

## 3. One knight, three lenses

**Build:** A 15 to 20 second AI short to post: one chunky armoured knight in the misty, red-ink painterly look, shown three times at 24mm, 50mm and 200mm, with big orange focal-length labels so viewers see how the lens changes the shot. Cut it fast like a launch film; it's a lens explainer and a showreel piece at once.
**From:** [Lens focal length guide](../topics/ai/2026-11-12-lens-focal-length-guide.md) + [Impossible Weapon animation](../topics/design/2026-11-12-impossible-weapon-knight-animation.md)
**Why it fits:** He's a video editor who saved both the lens trick and the art style for animations; this puts them together into content that shows off his eye.
**First step today:** Write three prompts that keep the same knight, pose and misty field and change only the lens line (24mm wide, 50mm medium, 200mm close-up with compressed background), and generate one still for each.
**Status:** suggested
