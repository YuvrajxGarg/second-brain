## 1. PINND: daily Mumbai pin drop

**Build:** A daily browser game on a dark night map of Mumbai where buildings glow gold. Each day gives five clues (a slang line, a famous vada pav stall, a film shooting spot, a local train station) and you drop a pin; the closer you land, the more points, up to 5,000, with a shareable result grid. Uses the PEAKD scoring loop, but on a map instead of a timeline.
**From:** [Flat Finder map](../topics/design/2026-11-21-flat-finder-map.md) + [PEAKD game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-peakd-game.md)
**Why it fits:** desi Mumbai humour plus a daily guess-and-share loop (Cricle, PEAKD), in the dark glowing-map look he just saved.
**First step today:** Fork the open-source flat-finder repo, swap the Gurugram data for OpenStreetMap building outlines of Mumbai, and hard-code five clues for day one.
**Status:** suggested

## 2. Worth a new tab, from my brain

**Build:** A "Surprise me" page on brainy.pxlvzn.in that turns his saved website-refs, tools and game-refs into a Tabworthy-style shelf: a dark grid of category cards with counts, monospace tags, one accent button that opens a random saved site, and a short line from each note's `why`. It reads straight from `/data/brain.json`, so every new save shows up on its own.
**From:** [Tabworthy site directory](../topics/tech/2026-11-21-tabworthy-site-directory.md) + [Perfolios portfolio directory](../topics/design/2026-11-12-perfolios-portfolio-directory.md) + [Second Brain repo](../topics/productivity/2026-11-12-second-brain-repo.md)
**Why it fits:** he saves directories like this but rarely revisits links; this makes the brain itself the hidden-gem site, in his dark single-accent UI.
**First step today:** Add `site/shelf.html` that fetches `/data/brain.json`, filters `kind` to website-ref, tool and game-ref, and renders the category cards plus one working "Surprise me" button.
**Status:** suggested

## 3. Rookwise blunder grid

**Build:** A shareable "year in chess" poster for Rookwise: every game becomes a cell in a dense dot-matrix grid, each move a tiny dot, square or cross coloured by quality (best, inaccuracy, blunder), animating from scattered dots into a heatmap of where you lose games. Exports as a PNG card with a serif title.
**From:** [Rookwise chess app](../topics/learning/2026-11-12-rookwise-chess-app.md) + [Neural network data viz](../topics/design/2026-11-15-neural-network-data-viz.md)
**Why it fits:** turns his own live app into something people post, in the generative glyph-grid look he saved.
**First step today:** In Rookwise, dump one imported player's move classifications to JSON and draw them as a static canvas grid (one row per game) before adding animation.
**Status:** suggested
