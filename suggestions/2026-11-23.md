## 1. Stem-split light rig

**Build:** A "Stems mode" for Runaway Light Rig. Instead of guessing notes from frequency bands of the full mix, drop in the four Demucs stems from Stm and give each its own light group and lane: drums fire the kick wash and strobes, bass drives the moving heads, vocals get the piano spot and halo, "other" runs the lasers. Cleaner onsets per stem means the charts feel hand-made, and watch mode becomes a proper light show for any song.
**From:** [Runaway Light Rig](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-runaway-light-rig.md) + [Stm stem splitter](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-stm-stem-splitter.md)
**Why it fits:** two of his own shipped PXLVZN/Labs projects talking to each other, in the red-and-black film-grain concert look he already built.
**First step today:** Split one song with Stm, then add a loader in Runaway Light Rig that accepts four files, runs the existing onset detector on each stem separately, and maps the drums stem to the strobes only.
**Status:** suggested

Cut a before/after split-screen reel from it.

**First step today:** Shoot one 10 s street clip, then remake it on the hosted Viggle (about $1 per 10 s at 720p) with the Sharma Sweets frame as the style reference before trying the open weights locally.
