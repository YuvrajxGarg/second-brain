## 1. Mogs XI

**Build:** A daily cricket face-off in the browser. Every day two countries are drawn; you pick five head-to-head duels from their all-time XIs (your batter against their bowler), each duel is simulated ball by ball with short commentary like 500-0, and you get a shareable 5-square result grid. Players are little pixel sprites on a flat pitch.
**From:** [500-0 game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-09-thechase500-cricket-chase-game.md) + [MyCountryMogsYours game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-09-my-country-mogs-yours.md) + [Pixel sprite search](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-09-pixel-search-sprite-database.md)
**Why it fits:** casual daily cricket game with one hook and a shareable result, the exact kind he saves and ships (Cricle, 500-0, Sixer), in chunky pixel art.
**First step today:** write a JSON of 8 countries with all-time XIs and three ratings each (bat, power, bowl), then code the single-duel sim in a plain HTML page and tune it until the odds feel fair.
**Status:** suggested

## 3. Bonk Court hands

**Build:** Swap Bonk Court's drag-to-shoot for a first-person arm: drag sets the IK hand target, the fingers curl around the ball, and release flicks the wrist to shoot. A clean flick swishes, a sloppy one clanks, and the arm's muscles tense on a full wind-up.
**From:** [Bonk Court game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-bonk-court-game.md) + [Procedural arm rig](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-11-procedural-arm-rig-demo.md)
**Why it fits:** both are three.js, the arm was saved "to reuse in my games", and it gives a shipped game a fresh feel instead of starting a new one.
**First step today:** clone the procedural arm repo, drop the arm into the bottom-right of the Bonk Court camera, and drive its IK target from the existing pointer drag.
**Status:** suggested
