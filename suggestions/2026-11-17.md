## 1. Stem wall

**Build:** A new mode for TV Stack where a song plays back as its stems instead of one mix. Drop in the four files Stm splits out (vocals, drums, bass, other) and each stem gets its own row of TVs that flash with that stem's energy, so the wall becomes a live breakdown of the track. Mute or solo a row by clicking it, and play your own keys over whatever is left.
**From:** [TV Stack toy](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-tv-stack-music-toy.md) + [Stm stem splitter](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-stm-stem-splitter.md)
**Why it fits:** Two of his own shipped things that already speak audio, joined into a small music toy with a black stage and red accent look he already owns.
**First step today:** Split one song with Stm, then add a file input to TV Stack that takes four files and drives one TV row per stem from a Web Audio AnalyserNode on each.
**Status:** suggested
