## 1. Card Safe dot grid

**Build:** A first version of his DIT offload checker as a Mac menu bar popover: plug in a camera card, pick the shoot drive, and every clip on the card shows as one dot in a dot-matrix grid. Grey dots are missing from the drive, white ones are copied, blue ones are copied and checksum-verified. One plain sentence on top ("131 of 131 clips safe on T7. OK to format.") and a single blue "Copy missing" button. Reuse Hop's transfer code for the copy step.
**From:** [DIT offload checker](../topics/tech/2026-11-13-dit-offload-checker-idea.md) + [Sleep alarm UI](../topics/design/2026-11-13-sleep-alarm-ui.md) + [Hop file transfer](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-hop-file-transfer.md)
**Why it fits:** His newest open idea, made for editors like him, in the dark dot-matrix, monospace, one-blue-accent look he just saved, and it slots into PXLVZN next to Hop and DWN.
**First step today:** Write a Python script that takes a card folder and a drive folder, matches clips by name and size, hashes the matches (xxhash), and prints missing / copied / verified counts. The grid UI comes after the logic is right.
**Status:** suggested

## 2. Ink stick Sixer

**Build:** A line-art mode for Sixer: the batter and bowler become wobbly black-ink stick figures on cream paper, animated from Mixamo batting and bowling clips. Retarget the clips onto one short stocky and one tall lanky character so the proportions are funny, and redraw the lines every few frames for the boiling hand-drawn wobble. Same timing game, a totally new look.
**From:** [Sixer cricket game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-12-sixer-cricket-game.md) + [Line art mocap workflow](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-13-line-art-mocap-workflow.md) + [Toyxyz retarget add-on](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-13-toyxyz-blender-retargeter.md)

**First step today:** Grab one Mixamo batting clip, retarget it in Blender onto a short and a tall rig with the Toyxyz add-on, then in three.js draw the skeleton's bones as thick jittered black lines on a cream background.
**Status:** suggested
