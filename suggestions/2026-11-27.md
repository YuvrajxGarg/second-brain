## 1. Candy cut: one-click transition panel

**Build:** An After Effects panel in the spirit of the "idk." tool: pick an intensity and a frame count, hit GO, and it drops a random stack of keyed layers between the two clips under the playhead (type cutout, shape stamps, pixel window, crop, white flash). Hit GO again for a new roll. Ship it as a PXLVZN tool next to the app hub, in your dark UI with one lime accent instead of the lilac candy look.
**From:** [Transition plugin idea](../topics/video-editing/transition-plugin-idea.md) + [PXL app hub](../topics/tech/pxl-app-hub.md)
**Why it fits:** It's your own saved idea, it's a tool you'd use on every edit, and it sells to the same editors PXLVZN already talks to.
**First step today:** Write one ExtendScript (`.jsx`, run from File > Scripts) that finds the cut at the playhead and adds 3 random shape layers plus a 2-frame white solid there. Once a roll looks good, wrap it in a CEP or UXP panel with the knob and GO button.
**Status:** suggested

## 2. Orbit dancer in pixels

**Build:** Shoot a 5-second clip of someone dancing, turn it into a 4D Gaussian splat sequence, then render it as a crisp pixel-art diorama floating on black while the camera orbits, one hue plus one accent. A 10-second loop for a Revolio Labs post, and a look test for using 4DGS in launch films.
**From:** [4DGS video test](../topics/video-editing/4dgs-single-video-test.md) + [Pixel-art gaussian splats](../topics/gamedev/pixel-art-gaussian-splats.md)
**Why it fits:** Two of your newest saves meet in the pixel-art-in-3D look you keep coming back to, and the output is a post, not a project.
**First step today:** Run ComfyUI SplatKit on one short phone clip and check the 16-angle contact sheet before worrying about the pixel render.
**Status:** suggested

## 3. Sharma Sweets in stacks

**Build:** Rebuild the Sharma Sweets shopfront (bottle-green sign, shutter, marigold garland, Lala ji) as sprite-stacked pixel art on a plain HTML canvas that you can rotate. Use it as a pixel "postcard" version of Munde Mode: a rotatable title screen or a street-stall level preview.
**From:** [Sprite stacking skill](../topics/gamedev/sprite-stacking-canvas-skill.md) + [Sharma Sweets 3D style](../topics/gamedev/sharma-sweets-3d-shopfront.md) + [Munde Mode game](../topics/revolio/munde-mode-game.md)
**Why it fits:** Desi street settings and pixel art in 3D are both core taste, and the new skill makes it an afternoon job instead of a 3D pipeline.
**First step today:** `npx skills add danielwh2/sprite-stacking`, then draw just the shutter-and-sign block as top, front and side pixel views and let the skill stack it.
**Status:** suggested
