## 1. Pixel Cricketer daily

**Build:** A daily browser guessing game: one international cricketer per day, shown as a heavily dithered, chunky-pixel portrait. Every wrong guess lowers the pixel size and dither strength, so the face sharpens over 6 tries, with Cricle-style green/orange hints (country, role, era) and a shareable emoji result grid.
**From:** [Cricle game](../topics/gamedev/2026-11-09-cricle-daily-cricket-guessing-game.md) + [Ladybug VFX app](../topics/design/2026-11-10-ladybug-vfx-app.md)
**Why it fits:** His favourite game type (daily cricket guessing with shareable results) with the dither/halftone look he saved Ladybug for, and the shader doubles as a first piece of the Revolio VFX feature.
**First step today:** Write one canvas/WebGL fragment shader with a single `reveal` uniform (0 to 1) that drives pixel size and ordered-dither strength, and test it on 3 player photos.
**Status:** suggested
