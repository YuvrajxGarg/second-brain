## 2. Player cam cutter

**Build:** A PXLVZN tool for editors: paste a match link, Dwn's engine pulls the video, you click one player once, and Roboflow's `reid` follows that player across every cut and camera angle. It spits out a timeline of every moment they're on screen plus an auto "player cam" cut (cropped and centred on them) ready to drop into Premiere. Dark near-black UI with one lime accent, a filmstrip with that player's segments lit up.
**From:** [Roboflow re-ID tool](../topics/ai/2026-11-10-roboflow-reid-tool.md) + [Dwn downloader](../topics/tech/2026-11-12-dwn-downloader.md)

**First step today:** In Python, run a detector plus `reid` on one 2-minute football or cricket clip, pick a player's crop from frame 1, and print the timestamps where the same embedding shows up again across cuts.
